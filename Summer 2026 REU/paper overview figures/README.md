# Paper Overview Figures

This folder stores final manuscript figures that describe the overall project,
rather than only one experiment.

Use this folder for:

- the full forecast-to-dispatch pipeline;
- the paper ladder that connects forecast, rolling horizon, scenarios, and oracle;
- the storage constraint summary;
- revenue and COVE calculation visuals;
- information-case comparisons such as baseload, causal, scenario, and oracle.

Experiment-specific figures are stored in their own folders:

- `../causal ridge regression/figures/`
- `../rolling horizon/figures/`
- `../different scenarios/figures/`
- `../oracle upper bound/figures/`
